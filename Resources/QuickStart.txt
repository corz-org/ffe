    ffe quick-start guide..
	
	You need ffmpeg installed to use ffe and ffe needs to know where ffmpeg
	lives. It's easiest to keep them all in the same directory.


    1.  In your browser, go here:  
		[note: these sites have nothing to do with me]

            https://www.gyan.dev/ffmpeg/builds/
            
        or here..
            
            https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds/releases

        Download the latest release.


    2.  In Explorer, unzip* the contents of the archive to a folder somewhere 
        inside your program files, e.g..

            C:\Program Files\corz\ffe\
    
        You should now have some files in that folder, mainly..

            ffmpeg.exe
            ffplay.exe
            ffprobe.exe

      If you already have an ffe directory, simply open the ffmpeg archive and 
	  drag all the files into your ffe folder.


    3.  Drop ffe.exe into the same folder, next to these files**, if it isn't
        there already.
		
		You are good to go.

    4.  Launch ffe.exe


    For more details about ffe usage, see the ffe page..

        https://corz.org/windows/software/ffe/


    Have fun!

    ;o)

    ffe at corz dot org


    *   If you need an zip program, go here: http://www.7-zip.org/
        
    **  You can keep them in different directories, no problem; ffe will ask you
        to locate your ffmpeg binary before you begin working. You can also drag 
        an ffmpeg binary directly onto ffe's drop window to instantly set a new 
        binary location.
